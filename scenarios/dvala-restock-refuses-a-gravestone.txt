#! nostage
# Dvala - a restock never happens around a player's gravestone.
#
# The sweep skips a due dungeon with a gravestone in its rooms and leaves the stamp alone. The
# sweep itself cannot be made due from a scenario, so this drives `dvala restock`, which asks
# the same Dungeons.HoldsTombstone question. Two claims:
#
#   1. With a gravestone placed inside the crypt, `dvala restock` from the entrance says refused
#      and names a gravestone.
#   2. (By hand, there is no remove verb.) Pick the gravestone up, run `dvala restock` again.
#      Expected: ok.
#
# Run it in SINGLEPLAYER, with Dvala's Crypts switch on. As in the sibling scenarios `place`
# drops from 8 m above onto the first collider, so under a low ceiling the stone can land on
# the roof outside every room box, and then claim 1 fails for a placement reason.
# UNTESTED in game: the prefab name Player_tombstone is assumed.

clear
god
ghost

mark
move 100
landed

location Crypt2
wait 3

enter
landed
wait 2

place Player_tombstone 2 0
wait 1

enter
landed
wait 2

mod dvala restock
printed refused yes
printed gravestone yes
wait 2

enter
landed

back
landed
