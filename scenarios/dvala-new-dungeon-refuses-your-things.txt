#! nostage
# Dvala - a new dungeon refuses to bury what a player built.
#
# A new layout is laid over whatever stands in the rooms, so a placed piece inside them must
# stop the run before anything is deleted. Three claims:
#
#   1. A workbench placed in the crypt is really there, counted from inside.
#   2. `dvala newdungeon` from the entrance says refused, and names the placed piece.
#   3. Back inside, the workbench is still there, and the log shows the dungeon was not
#      touched (no gen=1 line).
#
# Run it in SINGLEPLAYER, with Dvala's Crypts switch on, as in the sibling scenario. As there,
# `place` drops the piece from 8 m above onto the first collider, so under a low ceiling it can
# land on the roof and outside every room box; then the refusal does not fire and claim 2
# fails, which is a placement problem and not a Dvala one.

clear
god
ghost

mark
move 100
landed

location Crypt2
wait 3

enter
landed
wait 2

place piece_workbench 2 0
wait 1
count piece_workbench 1 8

enter
landed
wait 2

mod dvala newdungeon
printed refused yes
printed placed yes
printed gen=1 no
wait 3

enter
landed
wait 3

count piece_workbench 1 8

enter
landed

back
landed
